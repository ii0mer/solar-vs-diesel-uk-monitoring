# data/pvgis/

PVGIS Typical Meteorological Year (TMY) data for the four study sites. These files drive the step-1 sizing (`src/sizing.py`), the TMY reliability metrics and validation layer 2. The sixteen-year hourly series used for the multi-year check live in `data/pvgis_series/` (see its README).

|---|---|---|---|

## Data source

**PVGIS**: Photovoltaic Geographical Information System, European Commission Joint Research Centre.

## Provenance of the TMY months

The files were retrieved in May 2026 with `pvlib.iotools.get_pvgis_tmy` (the sixteen-year
series in `data/pvgis_series/` in August 2026). Matching each
TMY month against the 2005-2020 hourly series in `data/pvgis_series/` by ambient
temperature and wind speed identifies a unique source year for 43 of the 48 site-months
(RMSE below 0.4 degC against the matched year, above 3 degC against every other year);
the remaining five months (Southampton Nov, Birmingham Aug, Liverpool Jul and Oct,
Edinburgh Jul) match no year of 2005-2020 and therefore come from 2021-2023. The TMY as
served thus draws its month selection from the 2005-2023 satellite period (SARAH-3
era of the PVGIS service). The dissertation states this explicitly (Section III-A);
the final designs are selected on the 2005-2020 record itself, so no design rests on
the TMY alone.  
Reference year in timestamps: 1990 (standard PVGIS TMY reference year, not a real calendar year; the data are a composite of the most typical months of the 2005–2023 satellite period, as established above). Timestamps are on the hour, UTC.

Reuse terms: PVGIS © European Union, 2001–2026; the data are free to reuse with attribution to PVGIS, European Commission Joint Research Centre.

A file named `<site>_tmy_synthetic.csv` may appear locally: it is the offline fallback generated by `src/weather.py` when a real TMY file is missing, is not used for any reported result, and is not committed (`.gitignore`).

## Columns

| Column | Unit | Description |
|---|---|---|
| `time(UTC)` | | Hourly timestamp (index), nominal year 1990 |
| `ghi` | W/m² | Global horizontal irradiance |
| `dni` | W/m² | Direct normal irradiance |
| `dhi` | W/m² | Diffuse horizontal irradiance |
| `temp_air` | °C | Ambient air temperature at 2 m |
| `wind_speed` | m/s | Wind speed at 10 m |

## To re-fetch

```bash
python -m src.fetch_pvgis
```

Or to load a manually-downloaded CSV from the PVGIS web interface:

```bash
python -m src.convert_manual_pvgis
```

Visit https://re.jrc.ec.europa.eu/pvg_tools/en/#TMY to download manually.
