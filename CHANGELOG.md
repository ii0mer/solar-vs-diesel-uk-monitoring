# Changelog

All results quoted in the READMEs and in `docs/assumptions.md` are regenerated by `python -m src.make_all` and recorded in `results/numbers.json`.

## v1.4.0 — 2026-09

- Reliability-margin diagnostics for the final designs in `results/multiyear.json`: compliance margin (unmet hours in the closest compliant year, years within 5 h of the 87-hour limit, July–June season compliance), PV-series perturbation (x0.95, x0.98, x1.02) and 1 January 2005 start-state conventions (two-pass, full, half, floor).
- `worst_window` now finds ten-day windows that straddle 31 December (Liverpool 2006: 22 Dec–1 Jan, 157 h under the study chain, previously truncated at 147 h).
- `weather.get_or_create_tmy` no longer falls back to synthetic data silently (`ALLOW_SYNTHETIC_WEATHER=1` required; synthetic output cached under its own name).
- `results/numbers.json` gains `horizon` (10-, 15- and 25-year costing horizons at 5% and 8%) and `fuel_doubled` blocks.
- Figure fixes from the content audit (legend/label collisions, clipped labels, two-decimal cells near the 1% threshold, visit shares printed on the cost bars, deterministic ratio marked on the Monte Carlo plot).
- TMY provenance dates corrected in `data/pvgis/README.md`; 47 tests.

## v1.3.0 — 2026-08

- Two-layer validation of the PV chain against PVGIS (same-input hourly comparison 2005-2020 and TMY chain against the sixteen-year climatology), with the PVGIS 5.2 `seriescalc` hourly series committed in `data/pvgis_series/`.
- Sixteen-year reliability check (2005-2020, governing-year conditions) of every grid design under two PV conversion models; final designs now hold annual LOLP <= 1% in at least 15 of 16 years under both chains (500/2.0, 800/1.5, 550/2.0, 850/1.75 Wp/kWh).
- Economics re-based on the final designs: solar LCOE £6.52 to £7.23/kWh at 5%, diesel A2 £22.72/kWh, ratios 3.14 to 3.49 at 5% and 2.86 to 3.24 at 8%.
- Monte Carlo extended to seven sampled inputs with a diesel-capital multiplier tri(0.7, 1.0, 1.3); combined worst case now also stacks diesel capital at -30%.
- Simulation start-condition fix: every reported run is a second pass starting from the first pass's final state of charge.
- All ten paper figures regenerated (`figures/paper/`), including the 2 x 2 sixteen-year LOLP heat maps and the Edinburgh December 2010 worst-event trace; 38 tests.

## v1.2.0 — 2026-08

- Diesel comparator re-parameterised to a 2 kVA-class (1.6 kW) air-cooled set on an evidence-pack basis: Skarstein-Uhlen part-load fuel, energy-balance-derived A2 runtime, £250 auto-start controller, itemised visit costing.
- Review fixes: angle-of-incidence losses in the PV chain, battery state of health in the governing-year sizing, per-site visit-cadence break-even, re-sized combined worst case, price-of-reliability (0.1% target) designs.
- One-command pipeline `python -m src.make_all`; `results/numbers.json` made the single source of every quoted number.
- Paper figure suite (`src/figures_paper.py`) and load/degradation sensitivity rows with exact re-sized designs.

## v1.0.0 — 2026

- Interim model: hourly TMY simulation of the PV-battery system at four UK sites, LFP battery bucket model, explicit loss chain, two diesel comparator architectures, IEA/NEA discounted-cash-flow LCOE at 5% and 8%.
- Step-1 grid-search sizing on the PVGIS-SARAH2 typical meteorological year with a governing-year LOLP criterion.
- Monte Carlo LCOE uncertainty, battery-life sensitivity and discount-rate x battery-cost sweeps.
